Monitoring and change-control design

Data and reservingConfirm earned exposure and loss maturity; reconcile development, trend, currency and coverage. The current workbook cannot complete these checks.
Calibration and credibilityTrack frequency, severity, A/E, claim counts, effective loss counts and large-loss concentration before changing segment rates.
Customer and conductReview lawful rating factors and proxies, affordability, renewal practices, quote cliffs, referrals and complaints. No protected-group fairness conclusion is available.
Change authorityVersion the statistical model, calibration, money rules and commercial actions separately. Require independent review, sign-off and a tested rollback.
